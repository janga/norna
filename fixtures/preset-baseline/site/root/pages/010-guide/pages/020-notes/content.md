# Named notes

The workshop opens on Saturday.[^margin:opening] Visits are free.[^margin:booking]

Groups can arrange a longer visit.[^margin:groups] Read the shared policy.[^policy]

[^margin:booking]: Please book ahead for groups of more than six. The booking confirmation includes an arrival time and an accessible entrance map.
[^margin:groups]: A group visit includes time at each workbench. Allow extra time for questions and hands-on activities. The workshop team can adapt the route to the group, and will confirm the arrangement before the visit.
