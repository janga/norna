# BL-052: Docusaurus Migration Obstacle Inventory

## Status

**Deferred by maintainer decision on 2026-09-14.** The experimental decision
workflow is archived privately, not scheduled for completion. Maintaining its
classifications, decision tables, and coverage reports became too much work
relative to the practical migration help they provided.

The previously committed read-only `migrate:check` audit remains unchanged.
Retaining that prototype is not a guarantee of migration completeness or of
successful conversion. The uncommitted decision-registry and site-generation
extensions were preserved privately and removed from the working tree.

## Conditions For Resuming

Resume only when an owner has a real site to migrate and approves a bounded
trial. Select a few representative pages and inspect their source constructs,
not the subject matter of their prose.

For each concrete obstacle, identify:

- source evidence and the affected construction;
- the current Norna representation, if any;
- a readable fallback and any information or behavior it loses; and
- whether conversion, a focused Norna feature, editorial work, or intentional
  non-support is appropriate.

Build focused conversions and regression fixtures where the trial establishes
a need. Do not restart a general decision-management system as a prerequisite.
Keep competitor sources read-only; do not execute or build their projects.
