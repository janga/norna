# Sitewide Content


## Shared across the site {#intro}

This demo separates page content from elements that belong to the site as a
whole. Logo display settings, banners and footer come from shared files
and remain consistent as you move between pages.

Visit [Logo and navigation](/logo-and-navigation/) to inspect the logo and navigation model,
[Notices](/notices/) to inspect the banner stack and dismissal behaviour, and
[Footer](/footer/) to inspect persistent footer content and build information.
