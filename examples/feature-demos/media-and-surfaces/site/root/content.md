# Media and Surfaces
This site is a focused test bench for Norna's image and card blocks, sidenotes,
palettes and section-surface controls.

Use [Media blocks](/media/) to compare image stacks, carousels, card lists and
sidenotes. Use [Surfaces](/surfaces/) to inspect palettes and automatic section
surface sequences.

Logo handling, banners and footers have their own
[sitewide content demo](https://janga.github.io/norna/examples/feature-demos/sitewide-content/).
